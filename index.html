Bee, I don't always know how to explain what you mean to me, but I hope you know that you hold a special place in my heart. Having you in my life is something I don't take for granted, and no matter how hard things get, I'll always be grateful for the memories we share and the love we have. 🤍

I appreciate you for who you are, for the little things you do, and for the moments we share. Sometimes, it is the smallest memories that mean the most to me because they remind me how special it is to have you in my life. Loving you has taught me patience, understanding, and the importance of showing up for someone. I know neither of us is perfect, and I don't expect everything to be perfect. What matters to me is that we keep learning, communicating, and appreciating each other. I hope we continue making memories, laughing about little things, supporting each other's dreams, and growing into better versions of ourselves. I want you to know that your dreams matter to me, your happiness matters to me, and the person you are becoming is worth celebrating. Thank you for the memories we've made and for the moments that still lie ahead of us. Even an ordinary day can become meaningful when it is shared with someone special. I don't know everything the future holds, but I hope it gives us more reasons to smile, more lessons to learn, and more beautiful memories to look back on. You are my Bee, and this little corner of the internet is my way of giving you something made with care, just for you. Whenever you open this page, I hope it reminds you that you are appreciated and that the little things can sometimes carry the biggest feelings.

🤍 ✿ 🤍

With love,
Teddy 🤍
